# Red-flag patterns in small business ideas

Patterns that often turn out to be legal blockers. Each is a prompt to check,
not a conclusion: whether it applies depends on the jurisdiction and the facts.

| Pattern in the idea | What it usually is legally | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Taking money from friends, followers or the public and promising a share of profit | An investment offering or collective investment scheme; possibly unlicensed deposit-taking | Securities / investment rules, exemptions for small raises, whether a licence is needed |
| Holding customers' money before paying it out to others (marketplaces, group buys, escrow) | Payment services or e-money; client money rules | Whether a licensed payment provider can hold the funds instead |
| Charging customers automatically on a trigger (penalties, usage fees) | Recurring payments needing clear consent; possibly unfair contract terms | Card-scheme and consumer rules on authorisation and refunds |
| Lending, "buy now pay later", salary advances | Consumer credit | Credit licensing, interest caps, affordability checks |
| Reselling tickets, branded or limited goods above face value | Secondary ticketing rules, trademark and platform rules | Price caps, organiser terms, bans on resale of named tickets |
| Importing goods to resell, especially jewellery, cosmetics, electronics, toys, food | Customs, product safety, labelling, possibly counterfeits | Duties and declared value, CE/safety marks, trademark checks |
| Under-declaring value or splitting parcels to avoid duties | Customs fraud | Nothing to optimise: this is illegal as described |
| Health, fitness, nutrition or wellbeing claims; anything diagnosing or treating | Medical device or health claim rules | Whether the software or product counts as a medical device; permitted claims |
| Collecting personal data, especially health, location, children's or biometric data | Data protection (e.g. GDPR in the EU) | Lawful basis, consent, data processing agreements, special-category data |
| Scraping other sites or reusing others' content, photos, music or brand names | Copyright, database rights, trademark, site terms | Licences, terms of service, fair-use scope |
| Workers paid per task or shift but controlled like employees | Employment misclassification | Employment status tests, minimum wage, social contributions |
| Selling homemade food or cosmetics | Food hygiene / cosmetic product rules | Registration of a home kitchen, labelling, allergen rules |
| Alcohol, tobacco, vaping, gambling, weapons, prescription drugs | Licensed and age-restricted activities | Licence type, age verification, advertising limits |
| Reviews, ratings or endorsements the business writes, buys or filters | Consumer protection rules on fake reviews | Disclosure rules, bans on fake or incentivised reviews |
